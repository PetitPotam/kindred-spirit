# Pip, the name and the art: what you may do

The code of Kindred Spirit is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). This page is about
something else: the **names** "Kindred Spirit" and "Pip", **Pip as a character** (her look, her skins,
her personality and voice as the project writes them) and **the project's art**: the skins, textures
and icon in `mod/src/main/resources/assets/kindredspirit/`.

They belong to PetitPotam ([github.com/PetitPotam](https://github.com/PetitPotam)). You're welcome to
use them, generously, as long as it's for something kind.

## Go ahead, no need to ask

- Play with her, **stream it and make videos**, monetized or not.
- Draw her, write about her, make fan art, memes, skins and resource packs.
- Fork the code, change it, build your own companion from it, and call it Pip if you like.
- Share the mod and its art, as is or changed, with your friends or on mod sites.
- Write about the project, review it, teach with it.

## Please don't

- **Sexual or lewd content** of any kind with Pip, her look or the project's art.
- Content that is **hateful, harassing, cruel or meant to hurt** someone; Pip in gore or shock content;
  using her to bully or target a real person.
- Using her to **scam or mislead** people, to spread malware, or to collect people's data without
  their knowledge.
- Presenting your version as **the official Kindred Spirit** or as made or approved by PetitPotam when
  it isn't. Forks are welcome: just say it's a fork.

Pip is cozy, cheeky and kind. If what you're making fits that, you're almost certainly fine.

## The art's licence

The project's art (the files in `mod/src/main/resources/assets/kindredspirit/`, and the images
`scripts/make_art.py` draws) is **not** covered by the AGPL. PetitPotam grants everyone a free,
worldwide permission to use, copy, change and share it, in Kindred Spirit and its forks and on its
own, **for any use that respects the "Please don't" list above**. That permission ends for anyone who
uses the art in a way that list forbids.

## Questions

Not sure whether something is OK? Open an issue and ask. Seen Pip used in a way this page
forbids? Open an issue or contact PetitPotam through GitHub.
