# Pip's icon

`pip-icon.png` (512 x 512) is a picture of Pip taken in the game, not drawn: the slime skin, winking, rendered close
up by the mod in front of a plain blue backdrop (`mod/devscripts/icon-wink.txt`, run with `-PvideoSize=1920x1080`),
the backdrop cut out to transparency, and a little of the backdrop's blue kept in her see-through shell (the teal she
had in the game). Chosen by the project's owner on 2026-11-13.

Copies: `mod/src/main/resources/assets/kindredspirit/icon.png` (the mod, Mod Menu, the launcher profile, the video
end cards; 256 px), `web/src/img/pip-icon.png` (256 px) and `pip-icon-64.png` (the website), and the Modrinth project.
Pip's art follows TRADEMARKS.md.
