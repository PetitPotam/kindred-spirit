Roadmap

Pip grows one step at a time: singleplayer first, then friends on your LAN, then a first public release, and online servers last. The live roadmap is on GitHub, where every idea and bug is an issue you can follow or comment on.

  1. Singleplayer polish Making her the best companion in your own world: how she moves, speaks, remembers and helps.

  2. LAN with friends One shared Pip for everyone in your world, and a Pip who visits when a friend hosts.

  3. First public release Easier setup on more computers, newer Minecraft versions, and a release on a mod site. Done so far: the Setup app for Mac and Minecraft 26.3. Next: a one-line install, her brain in a Docker image, and hosting on Windows and Linux.

  4. Later: online servers Pip on multiplayer servers.
