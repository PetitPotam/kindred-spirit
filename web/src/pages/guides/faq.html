FAQ and troubleshooting

Questions

Does she change my world?

Only when you ask, and only within the power and style you chose. The mod adds no blocks, items or mobs and saves nothing in your world, so you can add or remove it any time. What she does (a torch, chopping, building) is done the way a player or a command would.

Does it cost anything?

The mod and her brain are free software. The AI is the one you choose: Gemini has a free tier, Ollama runs free on your own computer, and paid providers bill you directly with your own key or subscription. The dashboard shows each AI call with an estimated cost.

What leaves my computer?

By default everything runs on your computer except the AI you choose. When you talk to her, your words, a short text description of your game and the few memories that matter go to that AI provider. With Ollama nothing leaves your machine. Your voice stays on your computer with local Whisper, and her memories in ~/.kindred-spirit/memory/. The game and her brain talk over 127.0.0.1 only, with a token. The project itself receives nothing from the mod.

Can she be wrong?

Yes. What she says is generated by an AI and can be wrong. She's built not to invent facts: she answers from the game, her tools, her memory and the local knowledge base, and says so when she doesn't know.

Which Minecraft versions?

Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 (the newest) and 1.21.1, with the Fabric loader: one Kindred jar for each. Friends on LAN must use the host's version. Bedrock Edition isn't supported. On 26.3, her Minecraft knowledge base still assumes 1.21.1 for things added since, unless you set its version.

Can I use her on a server?

On a LAN world with friends, yes: see Friends on LAN. On a public server she can talk, find and lead (start your messages with her name), but she can't change the server's world. Proper online servers come later.

Can I host Pip on Windows?

Not yet: her brain and the Setup app are made for macOS. On Linux, the manual setup passes GitHub's install checks, but nobody has played there yet. Friends on Windows can join a Mac host with just the mod. Windows and Linux hosts are on the roadmap.

macOS says it can't check the Setup app for malware

That's expected: the app isn't signed by Apple, because that needs a paid yearly membership. Its code is all on GitHub. The Download page shows how to open it anyway, once.

I installed only the mod, and she isn't there

The mod is her body; her brain runs on your computer (or on a friend's, if you join their LAN world). In your own world, one chat message says so, with links. Set her up with Kindred Spirit Setup, or join a friend who hosts her. /kindred hint off hides the message.

Can I rename her?

Yes: her name is a setting in brain.toml, and on the dashboard. Pip is ours; yours can be anyone.

When something's wrong

SymptomWhat to do
She doesn't answer at all/kindred status shows whether her brain is connected. Open Kindred Spirit Setup (it wakes her), or run scripts/start.sh, then /kindred reconnect.
Something fails in the SetupPress the step's Copy a help prompt and paste it into any AI assistant, or into an issue: it has the step, your Mac and the exact error, with keys and personal details taken out.
She can't hear you (on a Mac)Start the game with the Setup's Open Minecraft, or scripts/play-mac.command: the launcher opened from the Dock has no microphone permission and records silence.
The AI check fails with 401 or 403The key is missing or wrong in ~/.kindred-spirit/secrets.env. Run the gateway's check to test every part.
No voice, or no speech-to-textIs the local tool running? scripts/start.sh says; logs are in ~/.kindred-spirit/logs/.
She's slow to answerThe gateway's log shows how long each AI reply took. A faster model, or Gemini Flash-Lite, helps.
The game feels slowerLeave quality on auto, or set low. Turn down her liveliness switches.
A friend sees a blue Allay instead of herTheir game doesn't have the mod, or not the same version as yours: Update Pip.
A friend gets "Incompatible" or crashesThey must start the fabric-loader installation for your Minecraft version (26.3 or 1.21.1), with Fabric API and the Kindred jar for that version.

Still stuck?

Look through the open issues, or open a new one. Include your OS, Minecraft and Fabric versions, the Setup's help prompt if you used it, and the logs (logs/latest.log in the profile's game folder, such as minecraft-kindred-26.3, and ~/.kindred-spirit/logs/brain.log). Remove any API keys before you paste.
