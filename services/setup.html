Kindred Spirit Setup

Getting Pip ready on this computer

Hi! This guide gets Pip, your companion in Minecraft, ready on this computer, one step at a time. Each step says what it does and what we recommend. Test it checks that the step worked. If something goes wrong, Copy a help prompt copies a message with the step, your system and the exact error, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant (keys and your name are left out).

Everything stays on this computer. Your settings, keys and her memories live in ~/.kindred-spirit. You can come back to these steps later from the dashboard.
